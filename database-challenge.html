Database Challenge

I was tasked with creating a query that:

  • Includes a subquery
  • Features formatted outputs
  • Sorts output by a value

Below is the table structure I used to perform the challenge with. It documents teams, players, matches, results and other details of the EURO 2016 football championships.

I have written a query with the objective of returning every player who was disciplined with a booking during the tournament, sorted by number of times they were booked. A subquery then filters only the players who played for a specific team - in my case, Portugal.

Scroll to the bottom of the page or click here to view the SQL code and the outcome of the query.

SQL Query

SELECT a.player_name AS "Player", COUNT(b.player_id) AS "Bookings"
FROM player_mast a, player_booked b

WHERE a.player_id = b.player_id
AND a.team_id =
(SELECT country_id
FROM soccer_country
WHERE country_name = 'Portugal')

GROUP BY a.player_name
ORDER BY "Bookings" DESC

*Note that country_name Portugal in the subquery can be changed to the name of any country that participated in EURO 2016, to show which of their players got booked during the tournament.
